Vernazza sul Mare

July 2026

There is a nervousness before you step into the sea, when you first walk into her presence. Her rocky edges. Her boundaries. Before a soft wave of unspoken delight.

Where do we sit?
Here?
No, perhaps over there.
Let's see what's beyond those rocks.

It's funny how we search for the perfect place to meet the sea, as if she isn't already everywhere around us.

They arrived fully clothed, one in a straw hat. After picking their way across the rocks, they settled beside two older women who had been there since morning.

They look around. Everyone is swimming. Everyone seems to be absorbing the sea, the warmth, the afternoon.

Do we feel safe now?
Safe enough.
A top comes off, slowly. Sunscreen goes on.
Can you do my back?
What about my face?
My arms?
Ready to be sun-kissed.
The straw hat stays firmly on.
Are people looking at me?
I'm looking at them.

Now she is lying down, quiet with a book. Beside us, the ladies' voices ripple in low, soft tones. A family of swimmers drifts out toward the other rocks - moving away from us, away from everything.

What does it mean to float alone on the sea?
To surrender your weight to something older than memory.
I wonder if the sea is only one great bowl of emotion,
where every tide returns what we thought we'd lost.

Leave your name at the riverbank for word when it shifts.
